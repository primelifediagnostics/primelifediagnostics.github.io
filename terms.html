Our services

3

Doctor review of your results

Your results are reviewed by a GMC-registered doctor. The review involves a clinical assessment of your results, with the information you give us at booking, and written comments on what your results show.

Please note the following about the scope of this review:

  • It is a review of blood test results. It is not a face-to-face consultation, a full medical assessment or a replacement for ongoing care from your GP or specialist.
  • The doctor can only comment on the results and information available. Blood tests are one part of a clinical picture, and a normal result does not rule out a medical condition.
  • Where the doctor considers it appropriate, they may recommend that you see your GP, attend an urgent care service or have further tests. We do not provide prescriptions or ongoing treatment unless we have told you so in writing.
  • The doctor’s comments are based on the information you provide, so it is important that this is complete and accurate (see clause 7).

Our results and comments are intended to support, not replace, your relationship with your own healthcare providers. We encourage you to share your results with your GP.
