Booking, Payment & Cancellation Policy

Please read before booking

Full payment is required when you book. We ask for at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Your statutory consumer rights continue to apply.

1. Booking appointments

Prime Life Diagnostics Ltd provides private blood testing and health checks at our clinic in Doncaster and through our nationwide home blood collection service.

Appointments can be booked online through our appointment page or by contacting us on 01302 203565.

All bookings are subject to availability and confirmation. Patients must be aged 18 or over and provide accurate personal information, including any information required to assess the suitability of their requested tests.

2. Payment

Full payment is required at the time of booking to secure your appointment.

The total price, including any applicable home visit fee, will be displayed or communicated before payment is completed. Your appointment is confirmed once your booking and payment have been successfully processed.

Please check that you have selected the correct test or health check package before completing your booking.

3. Cancellations and rescheduling

We understand that plans can change. We ask all patients to provide at least 24 hours’ notice if they need to cancel or reschedule an appointment.

If you cancel with at least 24 hours’ notice, you may request a refund or reschedule your appointment, subject to your statutory rights and any applicable terms relating to services already provided.

If you cancel with less than 24 hours’ notice, or fail to attend, we may consider a reasonable charge reflecting costs or losses actually incurred, where permitted by law and where the applicable terms were clearly communicated before booking. Any refund will be assessed fairly on a case-by-case basis.

We encourage patients to contact us as soon as possible if they cannot attend. Exceptional circumstances will be considered sympathetically.

Nothing in this policy removes any cancellation or refund rights provided by law.

4. Missed appointments and late arrivals

If you do not attend your appointment and have not notified us, your booking will be treated as a missed appointment.

If you arrive late, we will try to accommodate you where possible. However, we may need to rearrange your appointment if there is insufficient time to complete the service safely or appropriately.

Any cancellation or missed appointment charge will be subject to the fairness and legal requirements outlined in this policy.

5. Nationwide home visits

Full payment is required at the time of booking for home blood collection appointments, including the applicable home visit fee.

Please ensure that the address and contact details supplied are correct and that suitable access to the property is available at the agreed time.

If you need to cancel or reschedule a home visit, please provide at least 24 hours’ notice.

For cancellations made with less than 24 hours’ notice, or where the healthcare professional is unable to access the property or complete the visit, any charge or refund will be assessed in accordance with the agreed booking terms, reasonable costs incurred and applicable consumer law.

If Prime Life Diagnostics cancels your home visit, we will offer an alternative appointment or refund the payment due for services not provided.

6. Cancellations by Prime Life Diagnostics

Occasionally, we may need to cancel or rearrange an appointment due to staff availability, operational issues, clinical considerations or circumstances beyond our control.

We will notify you as soon as reasonably possible and offer an alternative appointment or an appropriate refund for services not provided.

We will not charge you a cancellation fee when we cancel your appointment.

7. Clinical suitability and laboratory delays

Some tests may not be suitable for every patient. If we identify a clinical or practical reason why a requested test cannot be performed, we will discuss the available options with you and explain any applicable refund arrangements.

Laboratory processing times can vary, and delays may occasionally occur outside our control. We will make reasonable efforts to keep you informed of significant delays and communicate results when they become available and have undergone the appropriate review.

8. Refunds

Approved refunds will normally be returned to the original payment method where possible.

Refunds will be handled in accordance with applicable consumer law and the circumstances of the booking. Where a service has already been provided or costs have reasonably been incurred, any amount retained will be assessed fairly and in accordance with the applicable terms and your statutory rights.

We will not apply a blanket non-refundable policy that overrides rights you may have under the law.

9. Online booking cancellation rights

Bookings made online or by telephone may be subject to statutory cancellation rights, including rights under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 where applicable.

Depending on the service and circumstances, a statutory cancellation period may apply. Different rules can apply where a service is requested to begin during that period or has already been fully performed following the required consent and acknowledgement.

Please contact us promptly if you wish to cancel under your statutory rights. We will assess your request in accordance with the applicable legislation.

If you have questions about this policy or need to change a booking, please contact us:
