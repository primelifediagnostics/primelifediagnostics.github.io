Privacy & Cookie Policy

Please read this policy to understand how we use your information and what choices you have.

Prime Life Diagnostics Ltd (Company Number: 16601360), a private limited company registered in England with its registered office at 9 Inglass Drive, Edlington, Doncaster, England, DN12 1DG (“Prime Life Diagnostics,” “we,” “us” or “our”), is the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. This means we decide how and why your information is used, and we are responsible for looking after it in line with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Our clinic is at 105 Scrooby Road, Bircotes, Doncaster, DN11 8JN. This policy applies when you use our website, book a test, visit us, have a home blood collection or contact us.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

1

Information we collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect:

  • Contact and identity details: your name, date of birth, sex, address (including the address for a home visit), email address and phone number.
  • Booking details: the tests you choose, your appointment date, time and location, and the status of your booking and payment.
  • Health information: your blood sample, your test results, and any relevant health information you tell us, such as symptoms, medication or medical history.
  • Communications: emails, phone calls and messages you send us (including by WhatsApp), and our replies.
  • Payment information: payments are taken through our booking and payment providers. This website does not collect your card details itself.
  • Website usage information: the pages you visit, your device and browser, your approximate location, how you reached our site (for example from a Google advert) and whether you click our call, WhatsApp, email or booking buttons. We only collect this using analytics and advertising cookies if you accept them (see section 7).

Where possible, please share health details with us at your appointment or by a secure method we provide, rather than in open messages or on social media.

2

How we use your information and our legal reasons

We only use your personal information when the law allows us to. The main reasons are:

  • To book appointments, collect and process your sample and deliver your results. We do this because it is necessary to provide the service you have asked for (contract). Your health information is used because it is necessary for health care purposes and is handled by people who owe you a duty of confidentiality (UK GDPR Article 9(2)(h)).
  • To contact you about your booking, results and enquiries. This is part of providing our service and is in our legitimate interests in running our business well.
  • To keep records and meet our legal, regulatory and professional duties, including requirements that apply to us as a regulated provider, and to deal with any safeguarding concerns (legal obligation).
  • To run, secure and improve our business and website (legitimate interests).
  • To measure how our website and Google adverts perform. We only do this if you accept analytics and advertising cookies (consent). You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • To send you marketing, only if you have agreed. You can opt out at any time.
3

Who we share your information with

We share information only where it is needed, and only the minimum necessary. This includes:

  • The accredited laboratories that analyse your samples.
  • Our clinical team and the doctors who review your results.
  • Partner phlebotomy services, where we arrange a home blood collection for you.
  • Providers that support our service, such as our online booking provider (Setmore), payment, email and communication providers, and the IT and hosting providers for our systems and this website.
  • Google, for website analytics and advert measurement, but only if you accept cookies.
  • Your GP or another healthcare professional, but only if you ask us to or agree to it, or where the law allows or requires it, for example to protect someone from serious harm.
  • Regulators, law enforcement, courts and our professional advisers, where we are legally required or permitted to do so.

We never sell your personal information.

Some of our providers, such as Google and our hosting and booking providers, may handle information outside the UK, including in the United States. Where this happens we rely on safeguards required by UK law, such as UK adequacy regulations, the UK–US data bridge or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

4

How long we keep your information

We keep your information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above and to meet our legal, regulatory and professional obligations.

Booking and clinical records, including test results, are normally kept for 8 years after your last appointment, after which we securely delete or anonymise them. Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are kept for a shorter period. Website analytics data is kept for the period set in our analytics settings.

5

How we keep your information secure

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including limiting access to the people who need it and keeping your health information confidential. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so please take care when sending us information electronically.

6

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information (we may need to keep some records, such as clinical records, to meet our legal and professional duties);
  • ask us to restrict how we use your information;
  • object to us using your information where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • ask for your information in a portable format, where it applies; and
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

To use any of these rights, email info@primelifediagnostics.co.uk or call 01302 203565. We may need to check your identity first, and we will normally reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have used your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

7

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. When you first visit our website we ask whether you accept analytics and advertising cookies. These are only set if you click “Accept cookies”. If you click “Reject cookies”, we do not load Google Analytics or Google Ads.

Name Provider What it is for Lasts
_ga, _ga_* Google Analytics Tells visitors apart and measures how the site is used, such as pages viewed and clicks on call, WhatsApp, email and booking buttons. Up to 2 years
_gcl_au, _gcl_aw, _gac_* Google Ads Measures whether visitors who arrived from a Google advert go on to contact us or book. Up to 90 days
pld_consent_v1 (stored in your browser, not a cookie) Prime Life Diagnostics Remembers whether you accepted or rejected cookies. This does not need your consent. Until you clear your browser storage

You can change your choice at any time using the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of any page, or by clearing cookies and site data in your browser. Google explains how it uses data at policies.google.com/privacy.

8

Third-party services and links

Our online booking form is provided by Setmore and appears inside our appointment page. Setmore handles the information you enter there and may use its own cookies, so please also read its privacy notice. Some parts of our website, such as scripts, icons and images, are loaded from other providers, which can see your IP address when your browser requests them. Our website also links to other sites, such as social media and the Care Quality Commission. We are not responsible for how those sites use your information.

9

Children

Our private blood testing service is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 through our website.

10

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed.
