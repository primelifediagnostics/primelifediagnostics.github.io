Before your appointment

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a GP referral?
No. Our private blood testing service does not require a GP referral. You can select and book directly with Prime Life Diagnostics.
How long does an appointment take?
The blood collection itself is usually quick. Appointments are designed to be efficient and typically take around 10–15 minutes, including preparation and aftercare.
When will I receive my results?
Most results are available within 24–72 hours. Some specialised tests may take longer.
Do I need to fast before my appointment?
It depends on the test you are having. Some tests require fasting while others do not. Please check the preparation information for your chosen test before attending.
Can I have my blood test at home?
Yes. We offer nationwide home blood collection. The current home collection fee is +£59, in addition to the cost of your chosen blood test.
